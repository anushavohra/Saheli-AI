INSTAGRAM SELLING GUIDANCE

Why Reels matter more than regular photo posts, with real data:
Across multiple large studies, Reels consistently outperform regular feed photo posts in reach and engagement, estimates range from roughly 22% to over 100% higher engagement depending on the study, with Reels also getting shown to non-followers, meaning new people can discover you, not just your existing followers. One consistent pattern across studies: smaller accounts benefit the most, accounts under 5,000 followers see notably higher engagement rates (around 3.8-9.8% reach rate) than larger, more saturated accounts. As a new or small home-based seller, this is actually in your favor.

Timing matters:
Most of a Reel's views happen early, roughly 65% of views and 61% of interactions happen within the first 3 days of posting, with day one alone accounting for about 30% of a Reel's lifetime views. This means the first few hours after posting are worth actively engaging with, replying to comments, sharing to your Story, since that early activity signals to Instagram that the content is worth showing to more people.

Ideal length:
Shorter Reels tend to perform better for reach, aim for 15-30 seconds for maximum watch-through, staying under 60 seconds generally. Attention drops fast, some data shows up to 50% of viewers drop off within the first 3 seconds, so lead with your most interesting moment immediately, don't build up slowly.

Use Product Tags:
Businesses using Instagram product tagging see a documented average 37% increase in sales compared to businesses that don't. Tag products directly in posts and Reels so customers can view details and pricing without asking. Keep it simple, 1-3 tags per post, overloading a single post with tags reduces its effectiveness.

Your profile is your storefront:
Use a clear profile photo (logo or simple photo). Keep your bio short and clear, state what you sell in plain words, e.g., "Handmade earrings, made with care. DM to order." Add contact details using Instagram's business contact buttons.

Use Story Highlights:
Create saved Highlights for things customers frequently ask about, like your catalog, how to order, or reviews. Keeps information accessible without repeating it in every DM.

Selling through DMs:
Since full in-app checkout isn't available to every seller, replying promptly and clearly in DMs is often how a sale actually closes. Keep pricing, sizes, and ordering info ready, and always share price directly when asked, don't make a customer ask twice.

A realistic posting rhythm:
A commonly recommended mix for small businesses is roughly 3-4 Reels per week, alongside a couple of carousel posts, use Reels primarily for reach and discovery, and carousels/photos for deeper engagement with people who already follow you.

Consistency:
Keep your posting style, lighting, and product photos consistent, same principle as your photography guidance, this is what makes a small home-based shop look established and trustworthy over time.
