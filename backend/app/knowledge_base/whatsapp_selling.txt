WHATSAPP SELLING GUIDANCE

WhatsApp Business has a free built-in Catalog feature, this is the single most useful tool for a home-based seller, and many beginners don't know it exists.

Set up a Catalog:
Instead of sending product photos one at a time whenever someone asks "what do you sell," the Catalog feature lets you build a mobile storefront directly inside WhatsApp. Customers can browse your full product range on their own, see photos, prices, and descriptions, without you repeating the same information over and over. It's free, available in the WhatsApp Business app, and supports up to 500 products, more than enough for a home-based shop.

For each product in your catalog, include:
- Clear photos (up to 10 images per product, use a few to show different angles)
- Price
- A short, clear description
- A product code, if you're managing multiple similar items

Organize with Collections:
If you sell multiple types of items, group them into collections, like "New Arrivals," "Bestsellers," or a themed set like a gift bundle. This makes it easier for customers to browse instead of scrolling through everything at once.

Keep your catalog updated:
Update it regularly, add new items, remove sold-out ones. A stale catalog with outdated products can confuse or frustrate returning customers.

Make your catalog easy to find:
Share it proactively, don't wait for customers to ask. You can link it in your WhatsApp status, mention it when replying to inquiries, or send it directly when someone asks what you sell.

Why this matters:
A catalog makes a home-based seller look organized and established, even without a website, it's often a new customer's first real impression of your business, so treat it with the same care you'd put into a shop window.
