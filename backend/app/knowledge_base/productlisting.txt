WHATSAPP SELLING GUIDANCE

Why WhatsApp works well for a home-based seller, with real numbers:
WhatsApp messages have a 98% open rate, compared to roughly 20% for email, meaning almost everything you send is actually seen. Around 66% of customers who start a WhatsApp conversation with a business go on to complete a purchase, a notably high conversion rate compared to other channels. 72% of consumers say they're more likely to buy from a business that offers messaging as an option.

Response speed matters more than most sellers realize:
Businesses that respond within 5 minutes convert roughly 3 times more often than those that take 30+ minutes. On the flip side, 56% of customers abandon a purchase due to slow responses, and 64% of customers report spending more with businesses that respond quickly. Customers on WhatsApp generally expect fast replies, most respond to businesses themselves within about 45-90 seconds, so slow responses stand out and cost you sales, this is one of the highest-leverage things a seller can control for free, no budget or tools needed, just responsiveness.

Set up a Catalog:
Instead of sending product photos one at a time whenever someone asks "what do you sell," WhatsApp Business's free built-in Catalog feature lets you build a mobile storefront directly inside the app. Customers browse your full range on their own, with photos, prices, and descriptions, without you repeating information to every new customer. It's free and supports up to 500 products.

For each product in your catalog, include:
- Clear photos (up to 10 images per product)
- Price
- A short, clear description
- A product code, if managing multiple similar items

Organize with Collections:
If you sell multiple item types, group them into collections, "New Arrivals," "Bestsellers," or a themed bundle. Makes browsing easier than scrolling through everything at once.

Keep it updated:
Update your catalog regularly, add new items, remove sold-out ones. A stale catalog with outdated products can frustrate returning customers, and remember, 87% of customers are unlikely to repurchase after inaccurate product information.

Make your catalog easy to find:
Share it proactively, link it in your WhatsApp status, mention it when replying to inquiries, or send it directly when someone asks what you sell.

Why this matters:
A catalog and fast, clear replies make a home-based seller look organized and established, even without a website. Given how directly response speed connects to actual conversion, treating WhatsApp replies with real priority isn't just good manners, it's one of the most measurable ways to increase sales at zero cost.
